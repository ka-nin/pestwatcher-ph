Reading this table. RMSE, MAE and R² score the continuous forecast directly; Accuracy, Precision, Recall and F1 (macro-averaged) score the Low/Medium/High band each forecast maps to after ETL thresholding. Only MAE reaches significance for either pest — for both BPH and RSB, the engineered model's mean error is significantly lower than the raw model's (p = 0.029 and p = 0.0004). RMSE and R² trend the same direction but fall just short of α = 0.05 with only five paired folds. Precision/Accuracy wins for raw are not significant and reflect how macro-precision and accuracy behave on a Low-dominated, imbalanced target rather than a real forecasting advantage.
